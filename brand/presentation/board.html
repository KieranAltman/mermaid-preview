What we heard

The brief

An infinite-canvas previewer for Mermaid diagrams, rendered by Pele and saved in the browser. For developers and product people who think in diagrams. Black, white and grey only — the tones themselves separate the parts of the mark.

preciselightstructureda little playful

Success: reads as a diagram tool without drawing a flowchart, nods to the name without a cliché mermaid, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour and in black / white / grey.

Final design

Branch Tail

A decision node splits into two branches that flare into a mermaid's tail.

  • One sign for both halves of the name: the diagram (a decision and its branches) and the mermaid (the tail)
  • Black, graphite and silver separate node, yes-branch and no-branch; the order of contrast stays the same on dark
  • Softened corners on a strict diamond: friendly, still exact
  • A heavier small cut keeps the idea at 16–32 px; one-colour versions hold through the gaps
